# GHAZI RADAR — product and development brief

Build an Arabic, mobile-first US-market research website and companion Telegram bot. Collect the broadest useful set of free, permitted data, prioritizing quality, coverage, provenance and freshness over raw volume.

## Research workspaces
- Small caps: early momentum, breakout setups, catalysts, liquidity, float and dilution risk.
- Stocks and indices: charts, technical direction, levels, invalidation and multi-timeframe context.
- Options: unusual activity research, spread/liquidity, expiry, volatility, Greeks and scenario risk. High upside is a hypothesis, never a promise.
- News: attributable filings, regulatory events and company catalysts, with original source and publication time.
- Evidence: prospective tracking, independent out-of-sample evaluation and hypothetical costs, with insufficient evidence shown explicitly.

## Shared website / bot contract
Use the same qualified evidence and eligibility rules. Show source time separately from report time, and distinguish WATCH, research scenarios and confirmed qualifying alerts. Do not turn popularity, confidence scores or indicative prices into execution claims. Keep V11 and deduplication protections intact.

## Design direction
Quiet navy surfaces, readable off-white Arabic text, restrained teal actions and amber caution. Red and green retain semantic meaning. Use comfortable line height, visible keyboard focus, touch targets at least 44px and responsive layouts. Preserve explicit data limitations beside results.

## Engineering priorities
Repair missing/stale artifact delivery; measure provider reliability; deduplicate same-source transports; review licenses and redistribution rights before integration. Prefer small maintained adapters over indiscriminate project bundling. No paid dependency or broker order submission is implied by this brief. New feeds must be tested before advertised as active.
