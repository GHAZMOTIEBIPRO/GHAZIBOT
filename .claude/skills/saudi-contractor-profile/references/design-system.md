# Design system — construction corporate editorial

## Design direction (proposed, subject to official logo)
- Editorial minimalism inspired by architectural drawings, structural grids, accurate line work and material textures.
- Restrained palette (if no brand book exists): carbon `#19262C`, warm ivory `#F4F1E9`, structural gray `#607078`, muted copper `#AD8053`; preserve adequate contrast.
- Use 65% light pages for comfortable reading and 35% dark high-impact interstitial or cover pages. Do not impose fixed proportions if the content dictates otherwise.
- Arabic fonts: choose actually installed, properly licensed fonts with proven Arabic shaping and embedded/export compatibility; suitable families include Noto Sans Arabic or Cairo if available. Confirm font metrics in exported PDF.
- Direction: Arabic right-to-left across paragraph, layout logic, numbering, tables, photo captions; LTR only for URLs, English names, project codes and email addresses where applicable.
- Use generous margins, a predictable 12-column or simpler grid, consistent baselines, numbered project cards, and one visual anchor per page.
- A4 portrait: 210 x 297 mm; aim at generous safe margins (typically 12–16 mm) and print bleed only when preparing a print-ready job with printer specs; do not crop crucial content.
- Avoid cliché skyscraper silhouettes, random city photos, gradient overload, excessive gold/black, large paragraphs, tiny captions, fake seals/certificates, decorative AI logos, and stock photos labelled as completed projects.
- Consider a cover with full-bleed real high-resolution project photograph, an editorial title block and restrained architectural line work.

## Page modules
A. Full image cover with brand title block.
B. Section divider: one architectural photo + number + concise headline.
C. Scope/services spread: illustrated service cards, uniform icon family.
D. Case study: hero image + caption + verified facts + execution scope.
E. Process page: clear six-step flow, minimal prose.
F. Certifications: document-backed only, with expiration/status where relevant.
G. Contact page: verified address, contact channels, QR codes verified to resolve to actual approved links.

## Image policy
- Genuine project photos outrank stock in credibility.
- Do not subtly recolor images in ways that misrepresent execution quality.
- Prefer varied photography of actual work: site management, structural stage, details and finished results.
- Non-project photographs must not be represented as the establishment's work.

## Export
- Real editable text and shapes in PPTX; PDF should closely match.
- Test Arabic ligatures, Kashida behavior, joined glyphs, punctuation, English terms within Arabic lines and text extraction.
- For print ask printer about CMYK conversion and trim/bleed; don't pretend the RGB PDF is print-certified.
