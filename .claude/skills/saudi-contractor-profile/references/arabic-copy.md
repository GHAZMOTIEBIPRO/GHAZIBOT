# Arabic technical-commercial copy discipline

## Voice and structure
- Formal, straightforward Modern Standard Arabic with a strong Saudi B2B register; short sentences and tangible nouns.
- Speak from proven capability. Use specific categories of work rather than broad unsupported assertions.
- Use 'مؤسسة' for the legal entity if the name is مؤسسة بن غازي للمقاولات. Never conflate business tenure with its manager's tenure.
- Do not invent founding year, staff headcount, project count, client names, awards, ISO certificates, Ministry classification, or financial capacity.
- For project captions distinguish 'أعمال منفذة' from 'مجالات خدمات' and from 'صور توضيحية'.
- Headlines should usually fit on 1–2 lines. Limit copy per page; shift detail to appendices when necessary.

## Claim ledger
Create an internal source ledger:
`claim_id | proposed claim | source filename/page | verification status | approved public wording`.
Unverified examples must be marked `معلومة بانتظار المستند` in internal drafts and OMITTED (or neutrally replaced) in public exports.

## Example neutral copy (not factual statements about a particular institution)
- "نعمل على تقديم حلول تنفيذية في نطاق الأعمال التعاقدية المعتمدة لدينا، من التخطيط إلى التسليم."
- "تبدأ جودة التنفيذ بفهم نطاق العمل ووضع خطة واضحة للوقت والموارد والسلامة."
- "الخدمات الواردة في هذا الملف توضح نطاق الأعمال الموثق بالمستندات المرفقة."
Use these only after verifying they accurately characterize the establishment's practices.

## Buyer questions profile must answer
What exactly do you execute? Where? What can you prove? What is your process? What distinguishes the project experience? Who do I contact to request a proposal?

## Translation
For bilingual output, translate concepts naturally and maintain consistent terminology. Use correct English legal designation only if confirmed from official English entity records; otherwise use an approved translation and clearly distinguish it from registered English trade name.
